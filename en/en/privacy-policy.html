Ischia Transfer Service S.r.l. (“Data Controller”) is committed to protecting users’ personal data in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (“GDPR”) and the applicable data protection laws.

1. Data Controller

The Data Controller is:

Ischia Transfer Service S.r.l.
Email: info@ischiatransferservice.it

2. Types of data collected

The website may collect the following personal data:

  • name and surname;
  • email address;
  • phone number;
  • information related to the requested service, such as date, time, pickup location, drop-off location, number of passengers, luggage and notes entered by the user;
  • technical data necessary for the proper functioning of the website and the security of digital services.

3. Purpose of processing

Personal data is processed for the following purposes:

  • to respond to information, availability or quotation requests;
  • to organize, manage and provide transfer and assistance services;
  • to manage customer communications, including by email, phone or WhatsApp;
  • to comply with administrative, accounting or legal obligations;
  • to improve service quality and website security.

4. Legal basis for processing

Depending on the case, data processing is based on:

  • pre-contractual measures requested by the user;
  • performance of a contract or requested service;
  • compliance with legal obligations;
  • user consent, where required.

5. Processing methods

Data is processed using IT, digital and organizational tools suitable to ensure security, confidentiality and protection against unauthorized access, loss, unlawful use or unauthorized disclosure.

6. Data retention

Personal data is stored for the time necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected and, subsequently, for the period required by applicable legal, tax or administrative obligations.

7. Data disclosure and sharing

Data may be disclosed, within the limits necessary, to:

  • collaborators, drivers, local partners and suppliers involved in organizing the requested service;
  • technical providers used for website operation, communication management, hosting, email and cloud services;
  • entities or authorities to whom disclosure is required by law.

Personal data is not sold to third parties.

8. Data transfers outside the European Union

Some technical services or digital platforms used may involve the transfer of data to countries outside the EU. In such cases, the transfer takes place in compliance with the safeguards provided by the GDPR.

9. User rights

Users may exercise at any time the rights provided by Articles 15 and following of the GDPR, including:

  • access to their personal data;
  • rectification of inaccurate data;
  • deletion of data, where provided by law;
  • restriction of or objection to processing;
  • data portability, where applicable;
  • withdrawal of consent, where processing is based on consent.

Requests may be sent to: info@ischiatransferservice.it

10. Complaint to the supervisory authority

Users have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority if they believe that the processing of their personal data violates the applicable law.

11. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated over time. Any changes will be published on this page.
