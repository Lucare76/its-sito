From Capodichino to Ischia

Naples Airport to Ischia Transfer

If you land in Naples and need to reach Ischia, we organize the transfer from Capodichino to the port and, if needed, all the way to your hotel. You have one point of contact and clear instructions at every stage of the journey.

How the transfer works

  • Starting point: Naples Capodichino Airport (NAP)
  • Destination: Naples Beverello port, then ferry to Ischia and hotel transfer
  • On-time pickup, luggage assistance and ferry timing coordination
  • Service also available for the return route from your hotel in Ischia to the airport

Why choose our transfer?

  • Real experience since 2006
  • Support in Italian and English
  • WhatsApp support and quick replies
  • Direct coordination with no unnecessary waiting

FAQ Naples Airport to Ischia transfer

  • Is the transfer private? Yes, it is an exclusive service for the traveler.
  • Can you also help with the ferry? Yes, we provide full assistance up to Ischia.
  • How long does the transfer take? About 25 minutes from the airport to the port, depending on traffic.
  • Can I continue to the hotel? Yes, we can coordinate the transfer all the way to your accommodation.
