Ischia port to hotel transfer

If you arrive at Ischia Porto or Casamicciola and want to reach your hotel straight away, we organize the final part of the journey in a simple way. You avoid improvised taxis, waiting at the dock and uncertainty about where to go as soon as you disembark.

How the transfer works

  • Pickup at the Ischia Porto or Casamicciola terminal.
  • Easy meeting on arrival, with clear instructions.
  • Luggage support during the main stages of the transfer.
  • Direct drop-off at your hotel, resort or villa.

Quick answers

  • How do I get from Ischia port to my hotel? If you book in advance, your driver will be there on arrival and take you directly to the hotel.
  • Can I book for a group? Yes, private cars, minivans and group solutions are available with luggage.
  • Does the service also cover Casamicciola? Yes, pickups are available from both Ischia Porto and Casamicciola.
  • Does the service help with luggage? Yes, practical support is provided during the main stages of the route.

  • Hotels in Forio: euro 30.00 one way
