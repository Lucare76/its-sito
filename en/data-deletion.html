Data Deletion Request

Users may request the deletion of their personal data processed by Ischia Transfer Service S.r.l. at any time.

How to request deletion

To request data deletion, send an email to: info@ischiatransferservice.it

  • Full name
  • Email or phone number used
  • Subject: “Data deletion request”

Processing time

Requests are handled within 30 days in accordance with GDPR.

Limitations

Some data may be retained to comply with legal obligations.
